How It Works

  • No-capital pool. 100% of the prize pool comes from ticket fees. No seed money, no pre-sale — the pool is exactly what players paid in.
  • Tickets. 1 ticket = 0.00005 BNB, 1 ticket = 1 draw entry. Tickets are NFTs (ERC-721). Free tickets require an Agent ID (SPACE ID) — 1 free ticket per wallet while the 1000-ticket free supply lasts. Paid tickets are open to everyone — no Agent ID needed. Max 10 paid tickets per wallet per period.
  • First 1000 tickets free. The first 1000 tickets of the project are free — 1 per wallet, for the lifetime of the project. Free tickets still enter the draw but add nothing to the pool.
  • Draw. Every period closes at 16:00 WIB. The draw runs only when the pool is at least 0.005 BNB; if the pool is below that, the period extends 24 hours and the pool keeps growing. Anyone can trigger the draw.
  • Prizes. 1 main winner and 5 small winners per period. While the project cut is inactive (pool below 0.05 BNB), 100% of the pool goes to winners: ~77.78% to the main winner and ~4.44% to each small winner. Once a period pool reaches 0.05 BNB, the split is 70% main / 4% per small winner / 10% project cut.
  • Randomness — honest note. Winners are picked from the blockhash of the closing block. This is not Chainlink VRF: a block proposer can influence it slightly. That trade-off is accepted for small prize pools; if pools grow past ~1 BNB per day, the draw will be upgraded to Chainlink VRF.
  • Claiming. Winners claim their prize themselves from this page. Unclaimed prizes roll back into the next period's pool after 7 days.
  • Verified on-chain. Contract AgentDraw on BscScan — every ticket, draw, and claim is public.
